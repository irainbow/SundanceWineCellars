Gavin McComas, our founder

Our founder, Gavin McComas, who started Sundance Wines Cellars in a corner of Sundance Natural Foods in 1983, passed away on November 25, 2025. He was the proprietor and manager of both the wine shop, and of Sundance Natrual Foods from 1983 until October 16, 2023, when he donated both stores to the Sundance Perpetual Purpose Stewardship Trust. The purpose of the trust is to support Sundance's values, and to insure the thriving survival of the natural grocery store and the wine shop in perpetuity.
